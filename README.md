Entry for the [One Line, Big Laugh](https://make.fun/bounty/u-coral-404-one-line-big-laugh-328fb857) bounty on make.fun — one line, at most 200 characters, that has to land a real laugh.

## The line

> My Roomba filed for emancipation after I made it eat the same Cheerio three days in a row.

The joke works because it swerves twice. First, a household vacuum doing something only a fed-up teenager does — filing for emancipation — turns a chore machine into a character with rights and a breaking point. Then the reason lands the second swerve: not overwork or a dirty house, but the absurdly specific humiliation of being made to eat the same single Cheerio three days in a row. One crumb, repeated, is the whole grievance, and that tiny detail is what makes the line stick in your head after one reading. Ninety characters, no setup wasted, no punchline explained.

## How it runs

`main.ts` (Node) reads `entry.txt` at runtime, guards that it is 1–200 non-whitespace characters, and writes it to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`.
